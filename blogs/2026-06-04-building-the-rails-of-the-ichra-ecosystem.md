ICHRA's rapid growth is outpacing the infrastructure supporting it, with Finch's VP of Product noting that the biggest risk to ICHRA's trajectory is whether the infrastructure behind it can keep up, particularly in automating payroll integration and eligibility processes across fragmented systems. The article explores how unified API infrastructure can remove the bottlenecks preventing ICHRA from scaling effectively across diverse payroll providers.
